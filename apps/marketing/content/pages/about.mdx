# About Homicasa

Homicasa is a modern property management platform designed to help landlords and property managers streamline their operations, automate tedious tasks, and grow their rental business.

## Our Mission

We believe property management should be simple, efficient, and accessible to everyone. Whether you're managing a single property or a large portfolio, Homicasa provides the tools you need to succeed.

## What We Offer

- **Smart Automation**: Automate rent collection, maintenance requests, and tenant communications
- **Financial Insights**: Track income, expenses, and get real-time financial reports
- **Tenant Portal**: Give your tenants a modern, self-service experience
- **Document Management**: Store leases, receipts, and important documents securely
